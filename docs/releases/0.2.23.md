# Equalizer 0.2.23 — a more useful assistant

Install: `INSTALL.md`, with `git checkout v0.2.23` and `EQ_VERSION=0.2.23`;
connectors `ghcr.io/thevidocq/equalizer-connector:0.2.23`. Updating from
0.2.22: `INSTALL.md`, "Update", then `deploy/compose/check.sh`. No
migration, no new setting.

## Changes you will notice

- **The assistant knows more of the site**: its status (connector,
  legacy application, certificate, isolation, last 7 days), every version
  of the map and what changed between two of them with its risks, and how
  the check of each version went.
- **It reads the guide**: ask how to do something in the panel and it
  says which page to open and what to press.
- **It finds the CMS version**: it said the WordPress version was not
  known although the report had it (a long list in the report pushed it
  out of what the AI read). Plugins, themes and libraries with their
  versions are there too; it says when one looks old, and never invents a
  vulnerability.
- New example questions in the assistant.

## For operators

- Only the panel and panel-api change. The efficacy study passed on every
  lab site.
