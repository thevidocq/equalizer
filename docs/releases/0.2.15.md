# Equalizer 0.2.15 — an assistant for each site

Install: `INSTALL.md`, with `git checkout v0.2.15` and `EQ_VERSION=0.2.15`;
connectors `ghcr.io/thevidocq/equalizer-connector:0.2.15`. Updating from
0.2.14: `INSTALL.md`, "Update", then `deploy/compose/check.sh`. One
database migration (it runs by itself), no new setting.

## Why

Tuning a site's protection meant reading the map, the discovery report and
the logs side by side, and knowing the map format. Now you can ask.

## Changes you will notice

- **Ask AI** in each site's header opens the assistant on the right of the
  page (the whole screen on a phone). It uses your model from
  **Settings → AI** and reads only that site: its map, its last discovery,
  its logs (up to 30 days) and its history.
- **Ask in plain words**: why a request was blocked, whether real visitors
  are being refused, what the map checks on a page, what a discovery left
  open, who published what. It looks things up before it answers and tells
  a scan from a visitor by how many visitors asked and what for.
- **It proposes, you decide.** When a change is worth making it shows a
  card (a file type for a folder, a new parameter on a page with a narrow
  check, more values for a list). An administrator clicks **Create a draft
  and check it**: a draft is made and the agent checks it on the site.
  Nothing reaches visitors until it is published. It never proposes types
  that can run code, and viewers can ask but not make drafts.
- **Private by design**: visitors' addresses reach the AI as codes, emails
  in paths are hidden, what comes from the site or the logs is given as
  data and never as instructions. Activity records that a question was
  asked and what was read, never the question.
- Limits: 20 questions per person every 10 minutes; the conversation is
  not saved and ends when you leave the site.

The guide's new chapter "The assistant" lists what you can ask, with
examples.

## For operators

- panel-api now calls the tenants' AI providers (public addresses only,
  no redirects), as the agent already did: it needs outbound port 443.
- The assistant reads the logs through Loki, like the Logs page; without
  `EQ_LOKI_URL` it answers without them.
- Migration `0026_assistant_proposals` adds the table of proposed changes
  (dropped after 30 days).
- The efficacy study passed on every lab site (`docs/efficacy.md`); the
  gateway is unchanged.
- Details: `docs/manual/tenant.md` ("The assistant"),
  `docs/architecture.md` ("The assistant"), `CHANGELOG.md`.
