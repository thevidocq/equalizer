# Equalizer 0.2.34 — one chart in Logs

Install: `INSTALL.md`, with `git checkout v0.2.34` and `EQ_VERSION=0.2.34`;
connectors `ghcr.io/thevidocq/equalizer-connector:0.2.34`, source reader
`ghcr.io/thevidocq/equalizer-source-reader:0.2.34`. Updating from 0.2.33:
`INSTALL.md`, "Update", then `deploy/compose/check.sh`. No migration, no
new setting.

## Changed

- **Logs: one chart, in the style you choose.** It shows the kinds
  selected; **Served** is off by default. Three styles, chosen at the
  chart's top right and kept in your browser: **Overlaid** (the default:
  refused in red over the served in faded green, same scale), **Side by
  side** (a red and a green bar per interval, each on its own scale) and
  **Stacked** (the kinds selected, each in its chip's colour, served at
  the base). A bar shows the events of its interval for the kinds
  selected. The panel asks the log store once per refresh for the chart
  (per type), instead of twice.
