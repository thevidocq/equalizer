# Equalizer 0.2.18 — your conversations with the assistant are kept

Install: `INSTALL.md`, with `git checkout v0.2.18` and `EQ_VERSION=0.2.18`;
connectors `ghcr.io/thevidocq/equalizer-connector:0.2.18`. Updating from
0.2.17: `INSTALL.md`, "Update", then `deploy/compose/check.sh`. One
database migration (it runs by itself), no new setting in `.env`.

## Changes you will notice

- **Your conversations are kept, for you only.** Nobody else reads them:
  not the other administrators, not the platform operator.
- **Ask AI** opens your latest conversation about the site you are on: you
  go on where you stopped, also from another device.
- **History** in the assistant lists all your conversations on every site;
  one of another site opens that site with it. **Delete** removes one.
- **New** starts a clean conversation for another subject; the old one
  stays in History.
- **Settings → Assistant**: how many days conversations are kept after
  their last message (30 by default, 1 to 365).
- A proposed change shows, in a kept conversation, the draft it became.

## For operators

- Migration `0027_assistant_conversations`: two tables and a column on
  `tenants`. Conversations are deleted with their user, their site or
  their tenant.
- Visitor codes now stay the same across restarts (derived from the map
  signing key and the tenant).
- The efficacy study passed on every lab site (`docs/efficacy.md`).
