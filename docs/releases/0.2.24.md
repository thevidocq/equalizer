# Equalizer 0.2.24 — the discovery says what it is doing

Install: `INSTALL.md`, with `git checkout v0.2.24` and `EQ_VERSION=0.2.24`;
connectors `ghcr.io/thevidocq/equalizer-connector:0.2.24`. Updating from
0.2.23: `INSTALL.md`, "Update", then `deploy/compose/check.sh`. No
migration, no new setting.

## Changes you will notice

- **While a discovery runs**, a line under "Running: N pages so far…"
  says what it is doing: exploring (and as which test account) with the
  page it is on, opening pages in the browser, reading the stylesheets,
  building the map, the AI studying the structure, checking visitors'
  requests. No more wondering whether it is stuck.

## Fixed

- **Continuous discovery's hour**: a schedule saved before you chose your
  time zone showed 03:00 under your zone's name while it ran at 04:00. The
  form now shows the hour it really runs at, and tells you to Save to
  keep it at that hour in your zone.

## For operators

- Agent and panel change. The efficacy study passed on every lab site.
