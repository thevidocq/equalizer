# Equalizer 0.2.16 — the assistant shows the facts

Install: `INSTALL.md`, with `git checkout v0.2.16` and `EQ_VERSION=0.2.16`;
connectors `ghcr.io/thevidocq/equalizer-connector:0.2.16`. Updating from
0.2.15: `INSTALL.md`, "Update", then `deploy/compose/check.sh`. No
migration, no new setting.

## Changes you will notice

- **Cards in the assistant's answers**, drawn by the panel from the real
  data, never written by the AI:
  - an **event**: time, request, status, the rule that refused it, the
    visitor's code, and **Open in Logs** on that request;
  - the **refusals of a period** by reason, with requests and distinct
    visitors;
  - an **entry of the map** with what it checks on each value.
- **Formatted answers** (Markdown): bold, lists, numbered steps, small
  tables, code. Still no HTML, no images, and links only to the panel's
  own pages.
- The **Logs** page opens filtered when a link carries the site, the
  search and the period (the assistant's **Open in Logs**).

## For operators

- Nothing to change. The efficacy study passed on every lab site
  (`docs/efficacy.md`); the gateway is unchanged.
