# Equalizer 0.2.33 — the security study on the checked draft

Install: `INSTALL.md`, with `git checkout v0.2.33` and `EQ_VERSION=0.2.33`;
connectors `ghcr.io/thevidocq/equalizer-connector:0.2.33`, source reader
`ghcr.io/thevidocq/equalizer-source-reader:0.2.33`. Updating from 0.2.32:
`INSTALL.md`, "Update", then `deploy/compose/check.sh`. One migration
(`0032`, applied by the panel at start), no new setting.

## Changed

- **The security study is written after the agent's check of the draft**,
  on the map as the check leaves it, corrections included. Until then the
  report says so; the study appears by itself when it is ready, with the
  version it was written on. A discovery without a draft is studied at
  once; a check that never ends releases the study three hours after the
  discovery.
- **The study respects Equalizer's choices.** The whole site is served,
  administration included, and who may reach it is the site's decision:
  the study no longer advises closing an area, an admin path or HTTP
  methods at the gateway. A field left free because the site needs those
  characters (quotes in a message, a rich-text field) stays free: the
  study no longer advises `no_sql_meta` there, it says what the site's own
  code must do. Its next steps are what Equalizer can do (narrow a field,
  cover an entry point precisely with the source code, a rate limit, an
  owner check) or what is outside it (update a component, read the code,
  a task on the site itself).

## New

- **Logs: served bars.** The chart shows, per hour (or 5 minutes, 6 hours,
  day), what was served in green and what was refused in red, each scaled
  to its own highest bar. A served bar shows that hour's served requests,
  as a refused bar shows the refused ones.

## Fixed

- **Discovery report, "Kept out"** shows how many pages were kept out,
  not the 200 the list keeps.
